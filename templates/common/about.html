About TailTales ❤️

TailTales is your little “pet life admin” helper — a simple tracker that keeps the important stuff in one place, so you don’t have to rely on memory (or random notes you’ll never find again).

Add your pets and save their basics like species, breed, age, weight, and a photo URL. Think of it as a neat digital folder for each furry chaos goblin you love.

You can also track appointments — vet visits, grooming, training, and more — and mark them as completed once the mission is done. TailTales also highlights upcoming appointments with simple reminders on the dashboard, so it is easier to spot what is coming up next.

Preventive care gets its own spotlight too: flea and tick treatments, deworming, heartworm, and other recurring care. When you record a new dose, TailTales automatically updates the next due date for you, helping you stay on schedule without turning into a calendar wizard.

TailTales gives you a private little corner to manage your pets, so the information you add stays with your account and is there for your own easy tracking and peace of mind.
