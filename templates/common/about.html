About TailTales ❤️

TailTales is your little “pet life admin” helper — a simple tracker that keeps the important stuff in one place, so you don’t have to rely on memory (or random notes you’ll never find again).

Add your pets and save their basics like species, breed, age, weight, and a photo URL. Think of it as a neat digital folder for each furry chaos goblin you love.

You can also track appointments — vet visits, grooming, training, you name it — and mark them as completed once the mission is done. No more “Wait… was that appointment today or next week?” panic.

Preventive care gets its own spotlight too: flea and tick treatments, deworming, heartworm, and other recurring care. TailTales stores the last given date and the next due date, so you stay on schedule without turning into a calendar wizard.

Everything you create is owner-only and requires login, so your pet data stays private. TailTales is built with Django, keeps things clean and validated, and comes with admin customization for easy management.
